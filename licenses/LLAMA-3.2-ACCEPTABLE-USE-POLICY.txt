LLAMA 3.2 ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Effective Date: September 25, 2024

Meta is committed to promoting the responsible development and maintenance of AI
for the benefit of all. As part of this commitment, we have adopted this Acceptable
Use Policy ("Policy") that applies to your use of Llama 3.2.

If you access or use Llama 3.2 in any way, you agree to this Policy. Use of Llama
3.2 is also subject to our Privacy Policy; by using Llama 3.2, you agree to our
Privacy Policy located at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.

Prohibited Uses:

We want everyone to use Llama 3.2 safely and responsibly. You agree you will not
use, or allow others to use, Llama 3.2 to:

1. Violate the law or others' rights, including to:
   a. Engage in, promote, generate, contribute to, encourage, plan, incite, or
      further illegal or unlawful activity or content, such as:
      i.   Violence or terrorism
      ii.  Exploitation or harm to children, including the solicitation,
           creation, acquisition, or dissemination of child exploitative content
           or failure to report Child Sexual Abuse Material
      iii. Human trafficking, exploitation, and sexual violence
      iv.  The illegal distribution of information or materials to minors,
           including obscene materials, or failing to employ legally required
           age-gating in connection with such information or materials.
      v.   Sexual solicitation
      vi.  Any other criminal activity
   b. Engage in, promote, incite, or facilitate the harassment or abuse of
      individuals or groups of individuals
   c. Facilitate the actual or threatened violence or harassment of individuals
      based on age, color, disability, ethnicity, gender, gender identity, legal
      immigration status, national origin, political affiliation, race, religion,
      sex, sexual orientation, or veteran status
   d. Promote, facilitate, or engage in the unauthorized or unlicensed practice
      of any profession including, but not limited to, financial, legal, medical/
      health, or related professional practices
   e. Collect, process, disclose, generate, or infer health, demographic, or
      other sensitive personal or private information about individuals without
      rights and consents required by applicable laws
   f. Engage in or facilitate any action or generate any content that infringes,
      misappropriates, or otherwise violates any third-party rights, including
      the outputs or results of any products or services using the Llama Materials
   g. Create, generate, or facilitate the creation of malicious code, malware,
      computer viruses or do anything else that could disable, overburden,
      interfere with or impair the proper working, integrity, operation or
      appearance of a website or computer system

2. Engage in, promote, incite, facilitate, or assist in the planning or development
   of activities that present a risk of death or bodily harm to individuals,
   including use of Llama 3.2 related to the following:
   a. Military, warfare, nuclear industries or applications, espionage, use for
      materials or activities that are subject to the International Traffic Arms
      Regulations (ITAR) maintained by the United States Department of State
   b. Guns and illegal weapons (including weapon modifications)
   c. Illegal drugs and regulated/controlled substances
   d. Operation of critical infrastructure, critical safety systems, or critical
      financial systems
   e. Creation of malicious code or malware

3. Intentionally deceive or mislead users, including use of Llama 3.2 related to the
   following:
   a. Generating, promoting, or furthering fraud or the creation or promotion of
      disinformation
   b. Generating, promoting, or furthering defamatory content, including the
      creation of defamatory statements, images, or other content
   c. Generating, promoting, or further distributing spam
   d. Impersonating another individual without consent, authorization, or legal
      right
   e. Representing that the AI is a human when directly and sincerely asked or
      otherwise enabling users to take actions predicated on believing output to
      be human-generated
   f. Generating malicious code, including malware, ransomware, viruses, etc.

4. Fail to appropriately oversee, monitor, and control AI-generated content.
