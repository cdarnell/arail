This lab studies consumer debt as a knowledge domain: what a balance transfer, a debt-consolidation loan, and a home-equity line of credit each are, how avalanche/snowball payoff strategies compare, and what distinguishes a vetted, NCUA-insured credit union from an unverified lender. Every factual claim is grounded in the World's cited public sources (CFPB, NCUA, IRS, and named institutions' own public rate pages). This World never ranks a personalized 'best option,' never presents a static figure as a live quote, and is not a substitute for a licensed financial advisor.

Every term in this World is grounded in a cited source.

_Answer only from the terms below. Every term lists its source. If a question cannot be answered from these terms, say the World does not cover it — do not invent._

### Credit Products

- **Debt Consolidation Loan** (`debt-consolidation-loan`) — A single loan used to pay off multiple higher-rate debts.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/answers/balance-transfer/
- **Hardship Program** (`hardship-program`) — A lender or counseling-agency plan that temporarily eases repayment terms.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-credit-counseling-and-debt-settlement-debt-consolidation-or-credit-repair-en-1449/
- **Personal Loan** (`personal-loan`) — An unsecured installment loan for general use, including consolidation.
  - Source: https://www.penfed.org/personal-loans

### Debt Types

- **Auto Loan** (`auto-loan`) — A secured loan used to purchase a vehicle.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/auto-loans/
- **Credit Card Debt** (`credit-card-debt`) — Revolving balance owed on a credit card.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/answers/balance-transfer/
- **Secured Loan** (`secured-loan`) — A loan backed by collateral the lender can claim on default.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/auto-loans/
- **Student Loan** (`student-loan`) — A loan borrowed to pay for education, federal or private.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/student-loans/
- **Unsecured Loan** (`unsecured-loan`) — A loan not backed by collateral.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/student-loans/

### Lenders & Institutions

- **Credit Counseling Agency** (`credit-counseling-agency`) — An organization that provides budget and debt-management counseling.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-credit-counseling-en-1451/
- **Credit Union** (`credit-union`) — Member-owned, not-for-profit financial cooperative.
  - Source: https://www.ncua.gov/consumers/consumer-resources
- **Field of Membership** (`field-of-membership`) — The eligibility requirement that determines who may join a given credit union.
  - Source: https://ncua.gov/support-services/credit-union-resources-expansion/field-membership-expansion
- **GreenPath Financial Wellness** (`greenpath-financial-wellness`) — A specific, named, NFCC-member nonprofit credit-counseling agency.
  - Source: https://www.greenpath.com/
- **Navy Federal Credit Union** (`navy-federal-credit-union`) — A specific, named, federally chartered credit union serving military and veteran families.
  - Source: https://www.navyfederal.org/loans-cards/personal-loans.html
- **PenFed Credit Union** (`penfed-credit-union`) — A specific, named, federally chartered credit union.
  - Source: https://www.penfed.org/personal-loans

### Retirement & Secured Credit

- **401(K) Loan** (`401k-loan`) — A loan borrowed against the borrower's own 401(k) balance.
  - Source: https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-loans
- **Adjustable Rate Mortgage** (`adjustable-rate-mortgage`) — A mortgage whose interest rate changes periodically after an initial fixed period.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-an-adjustable-rate-mortgage-arm-en-99/
- **Home Equity Line Of Credit** (`home-equity-line-of-credit`) — A revolving credit line secured by the borrower's home equity.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-home-equity-line-of-credit-heloc-en-135/

### Payoff & Consolidation Strategies

- **Avalanche Method** (`avalanche-method`) — Paying off debts ordered by highest interest rate first.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/answers/balance-transfer/
- **Balance Transfer** (`balance-transfer`) — Moving revolving balances to a card with a lower introductory APR.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/answers/balance-transfer/
- **Debt Management Plan** (`debt-management-plan`) — A structured repayment plan arranged through a credit-counseling agency.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-credit-counseling-and-debt-settlement-debt-consolidation-or-credit-repair-en-1449/
- **Debt Snowball Method** (`debt-snowball-method`) — Paying off debts ordered by smallest balance first, popularized by Dave Ramsey.
  - Source: https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/how-debt-snowball-method-works
- **Low Fee Consumer Finance Practices** (`low-fee-consumer-finance-practices`) — Comparing total fees and rates before consolidating, a theme consumer advocate Clark Howard emphasizes.
  - Source: https://clark.com/credit/best-low-interest-credit-cards/
- **Prequalification** (`prequalification`) — Checking likely loan terms with a soft inquiry before a formal application.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-kind-of-credit-inquiry-has-no-effect-on-my-credit-score-en-321/
- **Refinancing** (`refinancing`) — Replacing an existing loan with a new one, typically for a better rate or term.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/should-i-consolidate-refinance-student-loans-en-561/

### Terminology

- **Amortization** (`amortization`) — The schedule by which a loan payment splits between principal and interest over time.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-does-paying-down-a-mortgage-work-en-1943/
- **APR** (`apr`) — Annual Percentage Rate — the yearly cost of borrowing, expressed as a rate.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/answers/balance-transfer/
- **Credit Score** (`credit-score`) — A numerical score summarizing a person's credit risk to lenders.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-score-en-315/
- **Credit Utilization** (`credit-utilization`) — The share of available revolving credit currently in use.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/blog/credit-score-myths-might-be-holding-you-back-improving-your-credit/
- **Debt To Income Ratio** (`debt-to-income-ratio`) — Monthly debt payments divided by monthly gross income.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/auto-loans/
- **Deferred Interest** (`deferred-interest`) — Interest that accrues retroactively on the full original balance if not paid off in time.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/blog/you-could-still-end-up-paying-interest-on-a-zero-percent-interest-credit-card-offer/
- **Fixed Rate** (`fixed-rate`) — An interest rate that does not change for the life of the loan.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-a-fixed-apr-and-a-variable-apr-en-45/
- **Grace Period** (`grace-period`) — The window after a billing cycle during which no interest accrues if paid in full.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-grace-period-for-a-credit-card-en-47/
- **Hard Inquiry** (`hard-inquiry`) — A credit check tied to a new-credit application that can temporarily lower a score.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-kind-of-credit-inquiry-has-no-effect-on-my-credit-score-en-321/
- **Introductory APR** (`introductory-apr`) — A temporary reduced interest rate offered for a limited period.
  - Source: https://creditcards.chase.com/balance-transfer-credit-cards
- **Minimum Payment** (`minimum-payment`) — The smallest amount a lender requires paid each billing cycle.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/answers/balance-transfer/
- **NCUA Insured** (`ncua-insured`) — Deposits insured up to $250k by the National Credit Union Administration.
  - Source: https://www.ncua.gov/consumers/consumer-resources
- **Nonprofit Credit Counseling** (`nonprofit-credit-counseling`) — Budget/debt counseling provided by an accredited nonprofit agency.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-credit-counseling-en-1451/
- **Origination Fee** (`origination-fee`) — An upfront fee some lenders charge to issue a loan.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/do-personal-installment-loans-have-fees-en-2120/
- **Payoff Statement** (`payoff-statement`) — A lender-issued statement of the exact amount needed to fully close out a loan.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-payoff-amount-and-is-it-the-same-as-my-current-balance-en-205/
- **Post-Promotional APR** (`post-promotional-apr`) — The standard rate a balance reverts to after a promotional period ends.
  - Source: https://creditcards.chase.com/balance-transfer-credit-cards
- **Promotional Period** (`promotional-period`) — The fixed window during which an introductory rate applies.
  - Source: https://creditcards.chase.com/balance-transfer-credit-cards
- **Soft Inquiry** (`soft-inquiry`) — A credit check that does not affect a credit score, often used for prequalification.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-kind-of-credit-inquiry-has-no-effect-on-my-credit-score-en-321/
- **Transfer Fee** (`transfer-fee`) — A one-time fee (typically 3-5%) charged to move a balance.
  - Source: https://creditcards.chase.com/balance-transfer-credit-cards
- **Variable Rate** (`variable-rate`) — An interest rate that can change over time based on a market index.
  - Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-a-fixed-apr-and-a-variable-apr-en-45/
