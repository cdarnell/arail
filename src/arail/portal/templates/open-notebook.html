Open Notebook is a self-hosted NotebookLM alternative that runs as a Docker service. It lets you:

  • Organize multi-modal research content (PDFs, videos, audio, web pages)
  • Generate professional multi-speaker podcasts from your sources
  • AI-powered chat grounded in your research
  • Intelligent search across all your materials

Then set up the lab

./arail setup                  # generates encryption key
./arail start                  # or just reload this page and click Start
